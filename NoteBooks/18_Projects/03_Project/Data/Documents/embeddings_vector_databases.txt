Embeddings and Vector Databases
An embedding represents text as a numerical vector. Semantically similar texts can have nearby vectors according to a similarity metric.

Vector databases store embeddings and support similarity search. The workflow is to split documents, embed chunks, store vectors with metadata, embed a query, and retrieve similar chunks.

Metadata can include document, section, source, or page. Metadata filtering can restrict retrieval. Keyword matching can outperform semantic search for exact names, identifiers, and rare terms, making hybrid retrieval useful.
