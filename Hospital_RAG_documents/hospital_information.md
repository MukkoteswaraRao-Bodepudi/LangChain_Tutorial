# ABC General Hospital — Hospital Information

## Overview

ABC General Hospital is a fictional hospital created for the Hospital RAG learning project. The hospital provides outpatient, inpatient, emergency, diagnostic, and specialist services.

## Services

The hospital provides:

- Outpatient consultations
- Inpatient care
- Emergency services
- Diagnostic laboratory services
- Radiology and imaging
- Pharmacy services
- Physiotherapy
- Preventive health consultations

## Emergency Services

The Emergency Department operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Patients with urgent medical conditions should report to the Emergency Department. Emergency cases are prioritized according to clinical urgency.

## Outpatient Services

Outpatient consultations are available during scheduled department hours. Patients should register before their consultation unless they are receiving emergency care.

## Diagnostic Services

The hospital provides laboratory testing and diagnostic imaging. Availability of a particular test depends on the department and clinical requirement.

## Pharmacy

The hospital pharmacy provides prescribed medications during its operating hours. Medication availability may vary.

## Important Note

ABC General Hospital is a fictional organization created only for learning and RAG experimentation. It is not a real hospital and this document does not provide medical advice.
